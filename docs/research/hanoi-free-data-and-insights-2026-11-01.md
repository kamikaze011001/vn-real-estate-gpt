## Bounded collection and quality gates

1. **Source gate:** register advertised URL, robots URL, dated terms evidence, intended fields/use, retrieval limit and owner. Check robots at the exact origin before a trial. Robots permissions and successful HTTP access do not grant content reuse. A sitemap aids discovery, not licensing. Do not route through unofficial scraper APIs or bypass login/challenges.
2. **Small free trial:** begin with one or two RSS channels and a proposed 20–30 manually reviewed portal links across multiple Hanoi areas/budgets. This is a proposed operational sample, not market estimation. Measure Hanoi relevance, completed-resale precision, parsing contradictions, duplication and retrieval failures before adding sources. Use conditional requests if supported; stop on 403/429/5xx and respect retry guidance rather than expanding retries across every feed.
3. **Stable evidence:** store source ID/canonical URL, source publication/update time with raw date text and timezone, `observed_at`, first/last seen, event/reporting period, content hash where permitted, and field provenance. “Today” must retain the fetch date; bumped dates do not overwrite first-seen history. Missing source dates remain unknown.
4. **Unit scope:** require Hanoi building identity, completed status evidence and resale intent for approved inventory. Track each independently; rental, mini/collective/officetel, off-plan transfers, project bundles and nearby provinces need separate scope decisions or exclusion. Preserve original and normalized addresses; label building/approximate/unknown positions.
5. **Quantity semantics:** preserve raw price/area expression, currency, total versus rate/range, net/gross/unknown basis and fees. Do not compute a confirmed total from an ambiguous rate, divide unrelated range endpoints, or hide title/body/structured-field conflicts. Human review resolves contradictions.
6. **Duplicates and lifecycle:** distinguish source ad ID from candidate property ID. Similar building/area/price is insufficient to merge units. Keep uncertain cross-post clusters reviewable. A new ad is not necessarily new inventory. A removed page or failed fetch means source unavailable/unknown; only documented supplier evidence marks sold/unavailable. Recheck identity before treating a repost as a continuation.
7. **Separate freshness clocks:** retrieval time, advertisement date, asking-price confirmation, availability confirmation, document/event date and rights review are separate. Agree a review owner/window before buyers use real inventory; downgrade expired confirmations. Permission-revoked/withdrawn records leave active recommendations under applicable retention terms.
8. **Publication gate:** RSS context and portal lead metadata never silently become supplier-confirmed inventory. Keep visible source/date/limitations. Approved inventory additionally requires original supplier authority/display permission and traceable completion, availability and legal-claim evidence per pilot rules.

No paid dataset is needed to run this evaluation. Reconsider a licensed source only after observed gaps and required rights/fields are documented; no enterprise product is part of the initial plan.
