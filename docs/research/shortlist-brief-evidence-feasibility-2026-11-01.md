The [daily research projection](../../server/candidate-research.mjs) instead exposes source labels/expressions with `project-identity-unresolved`, unknown completion/legal status and no inventory eligibility. This note creates no registry mapping or evidence record. A government-hosted citizen complaint contains **both citizen assertions and an authority response**; hostname alone does not establish who owns each claim. Developer/manager notices above are **commercial first-party statements**, distinct from government decisions. The schema’s existing `official` category does not erase that provenance distinction. Unknown page dates (such as the OCT3A portfolio and completed-project classification) remain unknown and cannot be silently assigned this research date.
