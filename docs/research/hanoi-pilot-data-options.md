# Hanoi pilot: data options

Research checked 25 October 2026. Scope: completed resale apartments for owner-occupier buyers; manually reviewed, permitted listings. No providers contacted or listings scraped.

## Listing supply

| Candidate | Evidence and practical implication |
|---|---|
| Batdongsan.com.vn | Its [operating rules](https://batdongsan.com.vn/quy-dinh-su-dung) state that listing copyright belongs to the posting member and prohibit unlawful copying/use/distribution of protected content. Request a licensed partnership or obtain original material directly from authorized suppliers; attribution alone establishes no reuse right. |
| OneHousing | Offers a resale channel and [market reports](https://onehousing.vn/bctt/bao-cao-thi-truong-Bat-dong-san-quy-4-2025). Its [social-network terms](https://onehousing.vn/thoa-thuan-su-dung-dich-vu-mang-xa-hoi) require written permission for commercial reproduction and reserve intellectual-property rights. Those terms are not a listing feed license; ask which separate inventory/data agreement applies. |
| Nhà Tốt / Chợ Tốt | [Nhà Tốt terms](https://trogiup.chotot.com/nguoi-ban/dieu-khoan-su-dung-cua-nhatot-com/) require written permission for commercial reuse of content/databases and prohibit removing image watermarks. A disclaimer that outsiders may copy public posts does not grant permission. Owner consent alone may not resolve platform/photographer rights. |
| Direct agencies / owners | [Tân Long's Hanoi project page](https://bdstanlong.vn/east-ha-noi-skyline-co-linh-bv2750/) illustrates project information and seller inventory, but does not establish reuse rights or current availability. A directly supplied CSV/Sheet with explicit redistribution rights is a practical pilot option; agreement, suitable completed-resale stock, and update capacity remain unconfirmed. |

**API/feed finding:** Targeted searches and the inspected primary pages did **not establish a publicly documented, openly reusable listing API/feed** for these providers. This does not prove private partner feeds are unavailable. Internal endpoints or public pages must not be treated as licensed APIs.

## Market context and news

- **Ministry of Construction:** Its [Q2/2026 release](https://moc.gov.vn/vn/tin-tuc/1206/96449/thi-truong-bat-dong-san-quy-ii2026-nguon-cung-khoi-sac-tap-trung-thao-go-the-che-va-thuc-day-nha-o-xa-hoi.aspx) reports aggregate supply, inventory and successful transactions. Useful context, not unit availability or a Hanoi resale comparable. Search retrieved its text; direct fetch returned 502, so recheck before product use.
- **Hanoi Statistics:** [Official monthly socioeconomic reports](https://thongkehanoi.nso.gov.vn/tinh-hinh-kinh-te-xa-hoi) include a September 2026 release. Suitable for dated macro context; they do not establish individual apartment transaction prices.
- **One Mount / OneHousing:** Its [January 2026 Hanoi report](https://cdn.onehousing.vn/HD/Reports/Reports%20on%20MPI/OM_CMI_Bao_cao_thi_truong_bat_dong_san_so_cap_Hanoi_Q4.2025.pdf) distinguishes primary and secondary transactions and defines geographic coverage. Some primary-market coverage includes Văn Giang, Hưng Yên. Preserve period, geography and methodology; do not mix primary-sale figures into Hanoi completed-resale comparisons. Public download does not establish chart/data redistribution rights.

For policy/infrastructure updates, prefer the responsible ministry or Hanoi authority's original announcement; store publication and event dates separately. News is contextual evidence, never proof that an advertised unit is still available.

## What must be confirmed before ingestion

Recommended pilot route: a small directly permitted inventory, supplied manually, with a named reviewer and supplier update responsibility. Confirm rights to structured fields, descriptions, photos, map location and contact details; permitted ChatGPT display/caching; expiry/removal process; update cadence; and evidence of completion/resale status. Keep source URL, permission record, source date, last-check date and confirmation method.

**Asking versus transaction:** Advertised prices remain asking prices even after a seller confirms them. Label availability, asking-price confirmation, and documentary checks separately. Only call a price a completed transaction price when supported by appropriately permissioned evidence of the completed sale; an aggregate market report cannot validate an individual listing's price.
