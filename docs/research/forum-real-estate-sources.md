# VOZ and OTOFUN as Hanoi property sources

Checked **25 October 2026**. Scope: completed resale apartments for people buying a home in Hanoi. Primary forum pages, operator policies and first-party navigation were inspected. No accounts created, inventories scraped, people contacted or access controls bypassed. Public access observed through a research fetcher is not a production access guarantee or a reuse license.

## Recommendation

Use these forums first as **community evidence and discovery**, alongside separately permissioned listings and official project/planning evidence. Start with a small, manually reviewed watchlist covering the projects relevant to the three pilot buyers. Do not turn forum posts directly into available-property inventory or verified market statistics.

Priority order:

1. **OTOFUN project discussions**: seed two project threads and manually identify dated posts relevant to parking, management, access and living conditions. Keep a source link and a question to verify; obtain appropriate reuse terms before publishing derived content in the app.
2. **VOZ Northern-property discussion**: use the curated project index as a discovery aid, then follow each concern to the particular dated post and a corroborating source. Keep unsupported claims out of factual property fields.
3. **Sale posts on either forum**: treat as candidate supplier leads. Inclusion requires an independently obtained original submission, authority to supply it, permitted display, and a fresh availability confirmation. A public phone number or seller post does not establish those conditions.
4. **Automation**: evaluate RSS or a permissioned API/feed only after scope and rights are settled. No public documented API onboarding/key program or outbound content license was established in this review; that is not a claim that none exists.

The proposed manual watchlist is useful immediately for internal research and source evaluation. Publishing links, titles, excerpts, summaries, photographs or embeddings are separate intended uses to check; this note does not assert that a link-first design automatically licenses any of them.

## Rules, rights and access

### VOZ

[Terms v1.1](https://voz.vn/help/terms/) state an effective date of **5 October 2026**. They acknowledge third-party applications connecting via API; this is **not public API documentation or approval for our use**. The intellectual-property clause grants the operator hosting/display/backup rights to operate the forum, not an express license to our app. Terms prohibit unlawful collection and infringement, allow removal/moving of posts and do not guarantee continuous service. Public-sharing risk warnings are not redistribution permission.

[Privacy policy](https://voz.vn/help/privacy-policy/) covers the website and API, distinguishes independently operated third-party apps and describes collection of activity/content data. Our app needs its own minimal-data design and disclosure; the forum's policy is not permission to profile posters.

The [property-section moderator rules](https://voz.vn/t/noi-quy-forum-bat-dong-san.5147/) were posted **17 March 2020** and remain pinned in the inspected index. They require property details, discourage unsupported denigration and provide for spam/duplicate removal. Their older legal references should not be treated as current legal advice. A second pinned [commercial-area rule thread](https://voz.vn/t/noi-quy-khu-thuong-mai-cap-nhat-08-2024.420674/) could not be fetched in this check.

### OTOFUN

The [operator agreement](https://www.otofun.net.vn/threads/thoa-thuan-cung-cap-va-su-dung-dich-vu-mang-xa-hoi-otofun.1962611/), posted **9 June 2026**, covers both domains. It separates social discussion from marketplace activity and says account/posting criteria do not guarantee sellers or goods. It addresses intellectual property, privacy, unlawful collection, moderation and requests to delete/hide/de-identify material, including quotations. The footer reserves rights and asks for source attribution; attribution alone does not establish the scope of permissible reuse. No outbound license or public API onboarding was verified. Privacy/data provisions were inspected within this agreement; a separate `/help/privacy-policy/` fetch failed, so no separate policy was validated.

The [discussion-box rules](https://www.otofun.net/threads/quy-dinh-box-bat-dong-san.1056849/) were posted **11 April 2016**, remain linked/pinned in the inspected discussion index and prohibit sales, rentals, brokerage and disguised advertisements there. Commercial discovery belongs in the marketplace. This rule is not evidence that all remaining discussion is independent or verified.

### RSS, robots and reliability

Both sites expose first-party footer RSS links: [VOZ](https://voz.vn/f/-/index.rss) and [OTOFUN](https://www.otofun.net.vn/forums/-/index.rss). Requests returned an `application/rss+xml` content-type error in the research fetcher, so **feed contents, coverage, dates, pagination and update behavior were not inspected**. A [VOZ member thread dated 7 January 2021](https://voz.vn/t/moi-kham-pha-ra-rss-feed-cua-tung-box-trong-voz.209170/) also describes category feeds; that is a user observation, not official integration documentation. No permitted polling rate, service commitment or commercial reuse grant was verified.

Requests to [VOZ robots.txt](https://voz.vn/robots.txt), [OTOFUN .net robots.txt](https://www.otofun.net/robots.txt) and [OTOFUN .net.vn robots.txt](https://www.otofun.net.vn/robots.txt) failed in the research tool. **No robots rules were verified.** These failures do not establish that the sites prohibit access or have no robots file. Robots directives and technical RSS availability would not establish content rights in any case.

Public HTML was often readable with a JavaScript-disabled notice; individual thread requests sometimes failed while category/indexed excerpts remained accessible. Search snippets are discovery aids, not a stable production feed. No authenticated APIs, hidden endpoints or third-party reader credentials were tested.

## Proposed pilot ingestion policy

These are product recommendations, not claims that the forums grant these rights.

| Concern | Proposed handling |
|---|---|
| Provenance | Store source domain, canonical URL, thread ID, specific post ID when available, project/building ID, original post/edit date, checked-at date, evidence type, reviewer and permitted-use status. Keep thread creation, latest activity and claim date separate. |
| Rights | Keep discovered sources internal/pending until intended uses are cleared. Record permitted fields, excerpt/summary/photo/embedding rights, attribution, retention, update/removal obligations and allowed access method. Never assume a forum operator can license every poster's photograph. |
| Allegations | Human-review negative claims. Avoid repeating identifiable-person accusations; turn unverified building concerns into dated questions to check with official documents, management and direct observation. Preserve uncertainty and conflicting accounts. |
| Privacy | Do not import phone numbers, personal profiles, exact occupied-unit locations, identity scans or private messages. Retain only necessary source attribution; do not equate usernames with verified owners/residents or infer personal traits. |
| Spam and conflicts | Label commercial posts and disclosed interests; exclude bump-only replies, signatures, copied news and duplicate quotations from evidence counts. Account age, reactions and posting badges are not accuracy scores. Undisclosed interests remain unknown. |
| Deduplication | Normalize `.net`/`.net.vn` redirects and thread/post IDs. Separate a claim from its quotations and reprints. Keep multiple offers for one apartment as candidate duplicates pending verification; never merge solely on approximate area/price. |
| Price and availability | Keep forum prices as dated asking-price claims with original units. Never infer transactions, availability, legal status or immediate vacant possession. Placeholder and ambiguous values stay unknown. |
| Freshness and removal | Manually recheck each source before showing a community note; proposed weekly review during the pilot. Reconfirm inventory separately with its authorized updater. Tombstone removed/withdrawn sources and stop presenting derived claims; handle edits/corrections and removal requests. Do not create a full-text archive by default. |
| ChatGPT presentation | Offer a small “Thảo luận cộng đồng” source list tied to the selected project, with date, uncertainty and a direct source link. Keep it distinct from verified listing fields and official evidence. Do not derive resident consensus, sentiment scores or project rankings from this convenience sample. |

## Before automating

Ask the relevant operator, through its published official contact route, whether it supports our intended read access and display/summary use, a narrowly scoped RSS/API/feed arrangement, limits, pricing and removal notifications. Ask separately about contributor content and image rights. No outreach is authorized or performed by this research task. VOZ publishes a [contact form](https://voz.vn/misc/contact); OTV's operator contact information appears in the [OTOFUN agreement/footer](https://www.otofun.net.vn/threads/thoa-thuan-cung-cap-va-su-dung-dich-vu-mang-xa-hoi-otofun.1962611/).

If permissioned access is unavailable, keep the forum role at a reviewed source-discovery workflow within the permitted scope and prioritize direct supplier submissions for listings. Do not make the pilot depend on scraping, forum logins or an unverified feed.
