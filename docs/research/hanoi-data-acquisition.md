# Obtaining data for the Hanoi pilot

Checked 25 October 2026. Starting position: no inventory or supplier relationships, three prospective interview participants. Confirm their current Hanoi apartment-search activity during recruitment. Research only: no accounts created, suppliers contacted, or listing inventories scraped.

## Recommendation

Start with **original, directly permissioned submissions from owners or two small agency partners**, selected around the three buyers' actual budgets and areas. Obtain 15–30 useful completed-resale apartments before pursuing a citywide feed. This quantity and sequence are proposed pilot choices, not established supplier commitments. Keep acquisition manual until usefulness and supplier cooperation are demonstrated.

## Proposed acquisition sequence

1. Confirm each participant is currently searching for a Hanoi apartment; capture their budget, bedroom needs, work destination and acceptable areas. Use those requirements to define a narrow supplier request.
2. Prepare one submission Sheet and a written permission statement. Request original descriptions/photos, supplier authority, building/location, area basis, asking price and inclusions, completion evidence, availability, last confirmation date, and permitted public contact details. Avoid collecting identity-document scans in the public dataset.
3. After outreach is authorized, approach direct agencies/owners first; platform and enterprise requests can run separately. Offer attributed factual display and relevant buyer enquiries, with no paid ranking. Do not promise leads or imply a partnership before agreement.
4. Require an identified updater, price/availability reconfirmation and prompt withdrawal. Record source dates and verification methods. Seller-confirmed asking prices remain asking prices, not completed transaction prices.

## Supplementary routes

[TinixAI's dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/tinixai/vietnam-real-estates) claims 3.5 million June 2025–March 2026 records; its noncommercial license requires separate written commercial permission. Documented fields omit source URLs, coordinates and current availability. It is a historical research candidate, not established map-ready inventory; no sample audited.

[Government guidance](https://baochinhphu.vn/xay-dung-quan-ly-he-thong-thong-tin-co-so-du-lieu-ve-nha-o-va-thi-truong-bat-dong-san-102260101113407654.htm) distinguishes published aggregates from detailed data requested from the Ministry of Construction through official channels with costs. This establishes an inquiry route, not a live listing API or redistribution grant.
