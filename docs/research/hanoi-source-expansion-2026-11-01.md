# Expanded Hanoi apartment source assessment

Checked 1 November 2026 using first-party web pages and official documentation entry points. Research only: no login, supplier outreach, account creation, paid signup, collection activation or inventory publication. This extends [data acquisition](hanoi-data-acquisition.md) and [daily enrichment](hanoi-daily-data-enrichment.md); it does not establish rights to collect or publish any source.

## Decision

Expand discovery across the seven portal/agency sources below, while obtaining original inventory directly from consenting owners/agencies. Facebook is useful as a human discovery and supplier-recruitment channel, but no dependable, authorized unattended Facebook intake route was verified. No licensed public outbound Hanoi apartment feed was established for the seven portals. An API sold by a scraper vendor is not evidence that the underlying portal or listing owner authorized reuse.

The shared dataset should cover Hanoi across areas and budgets. Individual buyer constraints belong in search. Every candidate still needs the completed-resale scope check, identity review, permission and dated evidence before entering approved inventory. A public advertisement establishes an advertised offer, not current availability or legal-document verification. These are product recommendations consistent with the [pilot specification](../pilot/specification.md), not claims made by the sources.

## Facebook: separate Groups, Marketplace and Pages

### What is established

Meta's readable [first-party explanation of scraping](https://about.fb.com/news/2021/04/how-we-combat-scraping/) states that automation to obtain Facebook data without its permission violates its terms, including where humans can ordinarily see that data. It also describes rate limits and other anti-scraping measures. The article is dated 2021; it is evidence of Meta's stated rule, not a substitute for checking the currently applicable contract before implementation.

The current [Automated Data Collection Terms entry point](https://www.facebook.com/legal/automated_data_collection_terms) redirected to login/temporary-block content in this check. Its current full wording and effective date were **not verified** from a readable primary response. No automated Facebook access or rights to reuse posts, photos, identities, phone numbers or derivative data are established by this research.

### Practical Facebook intake proposal

Use a small human-reviewed watchlist after an authorized operator can see the chosen surfaces. Keep candidate URL, observation date and our own short factual label in the private review inbox. Do not mirror posts/photos/comments or harvest contact lists. When contacting suppliers is authorized, request original listing material, authority to supply it, permission for public display and an update/withdrawal contact. Their Facebook presence can support attribution, but the supplier submission should be the source of publishable inventory. This is a proposed workflow; it has not been activated.

## Prioritized practical intake

1. **Original supplier inventory first.** Prepare a reviewable request for Tân Long plus another Hanoi agency or consenting owners. OneHousing is a separate structured-partner candidate. Propose an original Sheet/CSV with stable supplier ID, building, area and basis, asking-price components, completion evidence, source and confirmation dates, availability, rights, update owner and withdrawal process. This note authorizes no contact and establishes no supplier commitment.
2. **Broaden the private lead inbox.** Manually sample Batdongsan, Nhà Tốt, Alonhadat and Muaban across Hanoi areas and budget bands; use Homedy to fill identified gaps after filtering project bundles. Begin with source links and minimal own labels, not copied bodies/photos. Establish the permitted collection/storage operations before scheduling portal retrieval. Keep duplicate ads linked to one candidate property only after identity evidence supports that relation.
3. **Add authorized Facebook human review.** Validate a few building/project groups and supplier Pages in an authorized visible session. Prefer original owner/agency submission over reconstructing Facebook content. Marketplace stays an unverified optional channel until its account/region experience and permissible route are established.
4. **Request explicit feed terms in parallel after outreach is authorized.** For portals ask whether they offer outbound listing API, delta feed, licensed CSV or partner syndication **to our app**. Require sample/schema, coverage, rights for each content type, rate/retention limits, withdrawal/deletion delivery, freshness responsibility and quote. No such portal product is confirmed here. GMap is the explicit enterprise dataset offering, but needs a separate suitability/sample review.
5. **Keep enrichment separate.** Match exact planning/project documents to buildings and contextual reports to periods. Official context cannot upgrade an advertiser's unverified legal or availability claim.
6. **Only then automate the permitted sources.** Run a bounded retrieval trial in the actual worker environment, compare complete samples against the originals, measure additions/duplicates/staleness/failures, and require review before publication. Source activity, retrieval time, asking-price confirmation and availability confirmation remain separate dates. Human-visible public access does not demonstrate reliable scheduled access.

## Access and permission audit

- The six portal `robots.txt` entry points tested—[Batdongsan](https://batdongsan.com.vn/robots.txt), [Nhà Tốt](https://www.nhatot.com/robots.txt), [Muaban](https://muaban.net/robots.txt), [Alonhadat](https://alonhadat.com.vn/robots.txt), [OneHousing](https://onehousing.vn/robots.txt) and [Homedy](https://homedy.com/robots.txt)—were inaccessible through the research tool. Their current directives were not verified. Robots rules would describe crawler handling, not establish permission to republish content.
- API/feed searches surfaced third-party scraper products and the first-party [Batdongsan map-service root](https://apimap.batdongsan.com.vn/). That root only identifies map services; it is not evidence of a supported listing-feed contract. No scraper service was activated or treated as authorized by the source platform.
- Full terms/reuse coverage is incomplete outside the readable Batdongsan and Muaban pages. No unreviewed site is presumed permissive. Facebook current terms and developer details need a readable official-doc check before implementation; no secondary copy is used to claim current API entitlement.
- Successful page retrievals can be cached. They establish discoverability and inspectability in this session, not current offer status, representative market coverage or unattended reliability. No supplier's “verified” label is promoted into our evidence status automatically.

## Remaining decisions and evidence needed

Choose the first suppliers and authorize any outreach separately; agree a freshness window and an operator; verify intended collection/storage/display rights per source; inspect actual feed samples and update/removal behavior; recheck inaccessible Meta and government originals; validate Facebook groups with authorized visibility. These gaps affect activation and publication, not the usefulness of the source shortlist above.
