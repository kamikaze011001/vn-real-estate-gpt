## Decision

**Alonhadat is the best technically demonstrated additional source for a bounded advertisement-reference adapter now.** Its current [Hanoi apartment category](https://alonhadat.com.vn/can-ban-can-ho-chung-cu/ha-noi), [robots](https://alonhadat.com.vn/robots.txt) and five linked individual detail pages returned HTTP 200 using ordinary Python Requests from this machine. It exposes stable advertisement IDs, detail-scoped numeric asking prices, areas, bedroom counts, source location/project labels and displayed dates. Use an original factual label and original link rather than copying marketing titles, descriptions, photos or contacts. This is an advertisement-reference intake recommendation; successful fetching and an ad date do not create verified completed-resale inventory.

The [category footer](https://alonhadat.com.vn/can-ban-can-ho-chung-cu/ha-noi) reserves website copyright and says partial or whole copying requires written consent. That is an actual stated copying restriction, which matters for mirroring its expression or pages. This research found no explicit automated-access ban on the inspected Alonhadat pages, no first-party public outbound feed/API and no affirmative license for a derived service. **Absence of an API or affirmative license is not evidence that every source link or independently expressed factual observation is forbidden.** The precise application of the footer to a modest numeric-facts-and-links index remains unresolved; no legal conclusion about that application is claimed. Keep the implemented scope narrow and attributable rather than converting this uncertainty into a claim that public browsing or all factual research is prohibited.

**Rongbay is a technically demonstrated fallback.** Its [Hanoi apartment category](https://rongbay.com/Ha-Noi/Can-ho-chung-cu-Mua-Ban-nha-dat-c15-t5.html), [robots](https://rongbay.com/robots.txt) and one detail also returned 200 through ordinary Requests. The current operating rules reserve intellectual-property rights and prohibit unlawful copying/use/distribution; no inspected clause specifically established a universal prohibition on an original minimal facts/link index. Its visible dates have weaker semantics, so Alonhadat remains the implementation preference. [Rongbay operating rules](https://rongbay.com/quy_che_rong_bay.html)

## Date semantics and permitted use findings

Alonhadat's current [posting instructions](https://alonhadat.com.vn/dang-tin-nha-dat.html) direct members to refresh existing ads to move them to the top instead of reposting. An older first-party [UP service notice](https://alonhadat.com.vn/tin-tuc-c7/trien-khai-goi-up-tin-sieu-re-sieu-khuyen-mai-id414.html), dated 31 March 2014, describes repeated refreshing and ranking effects. The historical offer is evidence that a refresh product existed, not verification of current prices/service limits. Neither inspected page proves whether today's `datePosted` HTML attribute is original publication or changes when refreshed. Store it as a source-displayed date with unknown original/genuine-edit semantics. Our retrieval timestamp never fills the original-publication, genuine-edit or supplier-reconfirmation fields.

For the strict existing freshness policy, a successful check within 72 hours satisfies the retrieval limb only. Unknown original/genuine-edit date and missing supplier reconfirmation mean an advertisement cannot be promoted automatically into fresh approved inventory. Preserve it as a dated unverified research reference or archive according to the existing schema; keep unknowns unknown. This quality distinction does not prevent acquiring actual advertisement-reference records.

Rongbay's [operating rules](https://rongbay.com/quy_che_rong_bay.html) state that copyright/IP remains with the company and prohibit unlawful copying/use/distribution. The [membership/privacy policy](https://rongbay.com/Quy-che-thanh-vien-Chinh-sach-bao-ve-thong-tin-ca-fp31.html) deals with member data; it is not an outbound contact-data license. The inspected [CT36 advertisement](https://rongbay.com/Ha-Noi/Ban-can-ho-chung-cu-CT36-Dream-Home-c15-raovat-34423215.html) exposes ID `34423215`, 4.9 billion VND, 56 m² and 2 bedrooms, with a `Thời gian` label of 1 November 2026. No original/update distinction was established. Visible UP/lifetime-ad services make a date-only freshness assumption inappropriate. [Rongbay apartment category](https://rongbay.com/Ha-Noi/Can-ho-chung-cu-Mua-Ban-nha-dat-c15-t5.html)

InfoNhadat is a useful counterexample to “no API means prohibited”: here there **is** an explicit prohibition. Its [terms](https://infonhadat.vn/dieu-khoan-su-dung/), effective 9 May 2026, specifically prohibit automated bot/scraper crawling without permission, alongside copying restrictions. They permit personal reading, short attributed quotations and link sharing, but also prohibit direct competitive use of content. The technically readable [The Legend stock page](https://infonhadat.vn/quy-can-chuyen-nhuong-the-legend) has agency IDs, dates, asks and bedrooms, but the explicit bot restriction makes it unsuitable for the proposed unattended adapter absent a separate permission. No further automated stock exploration was pursued after finding that restriction.

## Implementation handoff and remaining uncertainty

Implement Alonhadat as a separate portal parser over the existing bounded Requests/robots transport, with exact-host/path validation, small sequential discovery/detail caps and stop behavior on denial/challenge/429/5xx. Use synthetic fixtures rather than committed response snapshots. Public projection should contain only the agreed minimal factual reference, our label, source link, checked date and uncertainty; keep full source expressions and review metadata private as appropriate. Do not publish contacts, copied descriptions/photos or assert ownership-document validity, current availability, completion or independently verified price.

Check duplicate advertisement IDs against existing intake before adding records; an ad ID is not enough to merge different ads into one physical unit. Include area/project contradictions, relative/unknown dates, malformed pages, related-card bleed, removal/redirect and access denial in validation. Parent implementation and import results must be reported separately from this research; this note alone does not claim records were imported or a collector enabled.

Unresolved: exact legal application of Alonhadat's copying notice to the narrow derived index; original versus refresh dates; unit-level identity/completion/availability; actual deployment-worker and scheduled reliability; source withdrawal/retention obligations; representative yield across projects. No ChatGPT UI, MCP metadata or hosted behavior was changed by this research.
